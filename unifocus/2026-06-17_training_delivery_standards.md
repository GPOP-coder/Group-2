# Training Delivery Standards — HM Alpha Engagement

**Date:** June 17, 2026  
**Meeting:** Unifocus Training At HM Alpha (Corporate)  
**Context:** Corporate training overview meeting; Akram Chahin (Unifocus) briefing consultants on delivery expectations

---

## Status Letter / Recap Notes

### Purpose
Document progress, accomplishments, and outstanding items at end of each visit/milestone.

### Required Content
- **Accomplished:** What was completed during this visit
- **Pending:** What remains for next visit or follow-up
- **Blockers/Issues:** Any obstacles encountered

### Cadence
- **Not daily** — at end of visit/milestone only
- Delivered to client stakeholder (HM Alpha)
- Consultants cc'd on responses

### Tone & Audience
- Professional, structured format
- Clarity on what was done vs. what's left
- Some letters short, some long — match to scope of work

---

## Consultant-to-Property Communication

### Direct Communication Authority
Consultant handles:
- Property-level scheduling changes (time moves, date adjustments)
- GM/manager availability conflicts
- Training session logistics

Consultant **does not** need HM Alpha approval for these items.

### Notification / CC
- All communications with property are CC'd to HM Alpha stakeholders
- Keeps HM Alpha informed without requiring approval for every detail

---

## Visit Structure

### End-of-Visit Deliverables
1. Status letter (recap of accomplishments + pending)
2. Documentation of what was taught/trained
3. Any issues or recommendations for next consultant visit

---

## Context
- Training framework applies across HM Alpha portfolio
- Consultant acts semi-autonomously with property on logistics
- Status letters are the primary communication artifact back to HM Alpha
- Five pilot properties completed initial implementation (reference point for expected quality/depth)
