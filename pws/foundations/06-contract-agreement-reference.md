## HGM Associates — Letter Agreement Format (BTTR Lewis & Clark Interpretive Trail, Phase 1)

**Full agreement:** `BTTR/2026-07-16_hgm-lcit-phase1-agreement.md` (BTTR is a separate identity from PWS, but Pete signed this one as BTTR President and it's a clean example worth reusing structurally).

**Why this is a useful model:** A short, clean letter-agreement format for a landscape architecture consulting engagement — not-to-exceed fee structure, a Scope of Services exhibit, and a General Provisions exhibit. Worth drawing on when:
- **Rewriting Pete's own PWS ↔ Unifocus agreement** (flagged 2026-07-20, "soon" — see [[project_unifocus_contract_review]]). Current PWS rates are below market (see `05-pws-strategic-direction.md`); a rate/scope rewrite is also an opportunity to tighten the agreement structure itself.
- **Future PWS client contracts** generally — HGM's format is a reasonable template for how to separate the letter (fee, terms, billing cadence) from a Scope of Services exhibit (phased deliverables) and a General Provisions exhibit (liability, termination, ownership of work product).

**Structural elements worth borrowing:**
- **Not-to-exceed fee stated plainly in the letter**, with Additional Services requiring separate written authorization — clean way to bound scope creep without a rigid fixed fee.
- **Scope broken into numbered phases** (Preliminary Design → Draft Final → Final → Bidding → Construction Administration), each with its own bullet-level deliverables — makes it easy to see exactly what's included at each stage.
- **Explicit "Items Not Included" section** naming exactly what's excluded and how to add it (a written Amendment) — avoids ambiguity disputes later.
- **Payment terms spelled out concretely:** monthly billing proportionate to work completed, 30-day payment terms, 1.5%/month late fee.
- **Liability capped at the greater of total compensation or a stated dollar figure** — a General Provisions pattern PWS's own agreements could adopt.
- **Termination on 7 days' written notice**, with compensation for work performed prior to termination — clean, mutual, unambiguous.

**Not urgent** — Pete flagged this as "don't process now, just link for future reference" (2026-07-20). Revisit when actually drafting the Unifocus rewrite or a new client contract.
