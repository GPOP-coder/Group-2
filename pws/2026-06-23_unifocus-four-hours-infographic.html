Standard = Flexible Budget

Standard is not a fixed number — it's a ratio. When volume goes up, standard hours go up. When volume goes down, standard hours go down. Actual = Standard means you're on budget, regardless of absolute hours.

Guests Run Your Business

Guest demand drives everything. Volume drives the standard → standard is your budget. "We were busy" becomes 115% to standard. Measurement replaces opinion. Every manager can explain their number.

Two Conversations

CFO / Finance: Standard ↔ Actual — Are labor costs in line with volume? Am I on budget?

GM / Operations: Both rows — Did we plan right? Did we execute? Where did we diverge?
