## Company Description
Unifocus is a workforce management and automation platform specifically designed for the hospitality industry. The company provides integrated software solutions for hotel operations, emphasizing the connection between labor management and guest experience.

## Mission & Vision
**Mission:** Unifocus aims to help hotels "operate smarter — not harder" by providing tools that consolidate labor forecasting, scheduling, attendance tracking, housekeeping, guest requests, maintenance, and compliance into one platform.

**Vision:** The company believes hotels shouldn't have to choose between profitability and guest experience. Their goal is creating systems where "data drives every labor decision" and frontline teams align with business objectives in real time.

## Core Philosophy
The company emphasizes outcomes over features, focusing only on solutions that measurably impact labor costs, operational speed, staff engagement, and guest satisfaction scores.
