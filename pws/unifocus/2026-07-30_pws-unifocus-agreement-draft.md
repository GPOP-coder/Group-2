## 1. Term and Termination

This Agreement renews automatically every 3 months from the Effective Date unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current term.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon **30 days' written notice**. Either party may terminate for material breach upon **10 days' written notice and an opportunity to cure**, except that breaches of the confidentiality provisions in Section 6 may be grounds for immediate termination. Unifocus will pay PWS for all services performed and expenses incurred through the effective date of any termination.

## 2. Independent Contractor Status

PWS is an independent contractor, not an employee of Unifocus. PWS is free to provide services to other clients, including the direct-client engagements already carved out below, and nothing in this Agreement requires PWS's principal to work on a full-time or exclusive basis for Unifocus.

**Existing non-compete carve-outs preserved** — PWS may serve the following as direct clients without restriction under this Agreement:
- CTF BM Operations d/b/a Rosewood
- CTF BM Operations d/b/a Baha Mar
- Happy Hour Lessee LLC d/b/a Margaritaville Hollywood Beach Resort
- Davidson Hotel Company LLC d/b/a Davidson Hospitality Group

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential the other's proprietary information disclosed in connection with this Agreement and use it only to perform the services described here. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

## 5. Ownership of Work Product

Configurations, standards, and deliverables built specifically for Unifocus under this Agreement belong to Unifocus. PWS retains all rights to its own general methodology, frameworks, and training materials, including those developed independently of any specific Unifocus engagement, whether used before, during, or after this Agreement.

## 6. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability

Each party will indemnify the other only against claims arising from its own negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement — neither party indemnifies the other for the other party's own sole or gross negligence. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the greater of (a) fees paid to PWS in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) $50,000.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
