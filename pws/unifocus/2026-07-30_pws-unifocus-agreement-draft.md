**INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT**

This Independent Contractor Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of **August 1, 2026** (the "Effective Date") by and between **Unifocus (Texas), L.P.** ("Unifocus") and **Platinum Workforce Strategies, LLC**, a [Nebraska] limited liability company ("PWS"), and is executed by Peter Castellano as PWS's CEO and principal consultant.

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Unifocus and Peter Andrew Castellano, individually, entered into an Independent Contractor Agreement dated May 27, 2025 (the "Prior Agreement"); and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to replace the Prior Agreement in full with this Agreement, under which all services are performed by PWS rather than by Mr. Castellano individually;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT AND TERM

**1.01 Engagement.** Unifocus engages PWS, and PWS accepts engagement, to provide workforce management consulting, implementation, and training services as described in Article 2 (the "Services").

**1.02 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and automatically renews for successive three (3) month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

**1.03 Prior Agreement Superseded.** As of the Effective Date, the Prior Agreement is terminated and superseded in full by this Agreement. Any Services performed and unpaid fees accrued under the Prior Agreement prior to the Effective Date remain payable under its terms.

## ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES

**2.01 Scope of Services.** PWS will provide the Services described in engagement requests or statements of work issued by Unifocus from time to time, which may include workforce management system configuration, labor standards and KBI design, training, and related consulting across Unifocus client engagements.

**2.02 Independent Contractor Status; Non-Exclusive.** PWS is an independent contractor and not an employee of Unifocus. This engagement is non-exclusive and part-time in nature: PWS's principal is free to provide services to other clients, and nothing in this Agreement requires PWS or its principal to work on a full-time or exclusive basis for Unifocus. Without limitation of the foregoing, PWS may serve the following as direct clients without restriction under this Agreement:

- CTF BM Operations d/b/a Rosewood
- CTF BM Operations d/b/a Baha Mar
- Happy Hour Lessee LLC d/b/a Margaritaville Hollywood Beach Resort
- Davidson Hotel Company LLC d/b/a Davidson Hospitality Group

## ARTICLE 3 — COMPENSATION

**3.01 Fees.** Unifocus will pay PWS the fees set forth in Exhibit A for Services performed.

**3.02 Travel.** Travel days (transit time that is not itself working time) are billed at the rate set forth in Exhibit A. All other time spent traveling on Services-related work (calls, remote preparation, onsite work) is billed as ordinary working time.

**3.03 Invoicing and Payment.** PWS will invoice Unifocus monthly for Services performed, itemized by engagement, based on time tracked in Clockify or a successor system. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of invoice date. Amounts unpaid after thirty (30) days accrue a service charge of 1.5% per month. Unifocus will separately reimburse PWS for reasonable travel expenses (airfare, lodging, ground transportation, meals) in accordance with Unifocus's standard expense reimbursement policy, billed at actual cost.

## ARTICLE 4 — CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party will keep confidential the other party's proprietary information disclosed in connection with this Agreement and will use it only to perform or receive the Services. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 5 — OWNERSHIP OF WORK PRODUCT

Configurations, standards, and deliverables built specifically for Unifocus under this Agreement belong to Unifocus. PWS retains all rights to its own general methodology, frameworks, and training materials, including those developed independently of any specific Unifocus engagement, whether used before, during, or after this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 6 — INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**6.01 Indemnification.** Each party will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from and against third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, neither party indemnifies the other for the other party's own sole or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**6.02 Limitation of Liability.** Each party's total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement is capped at the greater of (a) fees paid to PWS in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, or (b) $50,000.

## ARTICLE 7 — TERMINATION

**7.01 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

**7.02 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) days after receiving written notice describing the breach, except that a breach of Article 4 (Confidentiality) may be grounds for immediate termination without a cure period.

**7.03 Effect of Termination.** Unifocus will pay PWS for all Services performed and expenses incurred through the effective date of termination.

## ARTICLE 8 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.01 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

**8.02 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt, or by nationally recognized courier, to the addresses on file for each party's signatory below.

**8.03 Entire Agreement; Amendment.** This Agreement, including Exhibit A, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements on that subject, including the Prior Agreement. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

**8.04 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except that PWS may perform Services through its principal, Peter Castellano.
