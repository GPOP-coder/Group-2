# PWS Pre-Training Checklist — Property Preparation

**Purpose:** Prepare for training engagement before first contact with property  
**Timing:** 3–4 weeks before training start date  
**Outcome:** Property profile complete, scope confirmed, intro email ready to send

---

## PHASE 1: RESEARCH & INTELLIGENCE (Days 1–3)

### Property Contacts
- [ ] Identify General Manager (primary contact)
- [ ] Identify Director of Operations / Director of Rooms / Director of F&B (who owns labor management?)
- [ ] Identify Finance Manager or Controller (stakeholder for cost/budgeting)
- [ ] Identify HR Director (employee data, compliance)
- [ ] Get email addresses and phone numbers for all above
- [ ] **Source:** Email from Unifocus contact (Steve Carrell, Ralph Varble, John Grech, etc.)

**Notes:**
- Primary contact should be GM or senior ops
- Have backup contact if GM unavailable during training
- Finance/HR may not attend training but need to know timeline

### Property Profile
- [ ] Property name and location
- [ ] Property size (number of rooms, outlets, departments)
- [ ] Operating model (full-service hotel, resort, casino, etc.)
- [ ] Labor structure (how many departments? major cost centers?)
- [ ] Current labor management system (what are they using now?)
- [ ] Current pain points (why are they implementing Unifocus?)
- [ ] Go-live deadline (when do they need to be live?)
- [ ] **Source:** Unifocus account team or property website

**Notes:**
- Size determines complexity (50-room hotel ≠ 400-room resort)
- Labor structure determines training customization
- Pain points help frame training (efficiency, visibility, cost control, etc.)

### Property Website
- [ ] Review property home page
- [ ] Identify key outlets/departments from website (F&B concepts, outlets, room types)
- [ ] Note any special programming (casino, group business, spa, etc.)
- [ ] Look for seasonal patterns (beach resort vs. downtown)
- [ ] **Reason:** Understand property context; use real examples in training

**Notes:**
- Website often lists amenities = departments needing schedules
- Special operations (casino, high-volume events) = unique labor models
- Geography/seasonality = forecast complexity

### LinkedIn Research
- [ ] Search property name; find GM and key management
- [ ] Review their backgrounds (casino experience? hotel chains? union properties?)
- [ ] Note any relevant management certifications or experience
- [ ] Look for company announcements (new owner, expansion, renovations = context for implementation)
- [ ] **Reason:** Understand team's management philosophy and sophistication

**Notes:**
- Union properties require different Labor Rules setup
- Casino background = complex shift patterns
- New ownership = urgency around cost control
- Recent renovations = property reorganization (new departments?)

## PHASE 2: SCOPE CLARIFICATION (Days 3–5)

### Planning & Scheduling vs. T&A Integration

**Key Question:** Is this P&S only, or P&S + Time & Attendance integration?

**Planning & Scheduling (P&S) Only:**
- Manager builds schedules in Unifocus
- Employees clock in/out on existing system (badge readers, Kronos, etc.)
- Admin manually enters actual hours into Unifocus (or imports via file)
- **Scope:** Forecast, Planning, Scheduling, Labor Standards, Reporting
- **Training:** Manager (3 hr) + Admin (6 hr) as planned
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Timeline:** Faster implementation

**P&S + Time & Attendance (T&A) Integration:**
- Manager builds schedules in Unifocus
- Employees clock in/out on mobile app or property terminal
- Actual hours flow automatically from T&A to Unifocus
- **Scope:** Everything above PLUS T&A system setup, mobile configuration, reconciliation
- **Training:** Manager (3 hr) + Admin (6 hr) PLUS 2–3 additional hours on T&A integration and mobile
- **Complexity:** High
- **Timeline:** Longer implementation; T&A testing required

**How to Determine:**
- Ask: "Is Unifocus handling time & attendance, or just labor planning?"
- Or: "Will employees clock in through Unifocus mobile, or your existing system?"
- Or: "Does your current T&A system integrate with Unifocus, or are we running separately?"

**Action:**
- [ ] Confirm with Unifocus account team or property GM: P&S only or P&S + T&A?
- [ ] If T&A: which T&A system? (Kronos, Dayforce, etc.)
- [ ] If T&A: is mobile app part of implementation?
- [ ] **Document answer; adjust training scope accordingly**

## PHASE 3: PREPARATION FOR INTRO EMAIL (Days 5–7)

### Information to Gather Before Email

- [ ] Confirmed training dates (coordinate with account team + property availability)
- [ ] Training format (in-person at property, remote, hybrid?)
- [ ] Training times (match property availability, manager schedules)
- [ ] Location details (if in-person: conference room, AV setup confirmation)
- [ ] Teams link (if remote: Microsoft Teams meeting created and confirmed)
- [ ] Trainer name and contact info (you or co-trainer?)
- [ ] All training materials ready to attach (Manager Guide, Admin Guide, Smart Cards, video links)
- [ ] Pre-training checklist for property ready to send (separate email, 2 weeks before)

### Final Decisions

- [ ] **Training Scope:** P&S only, or P&S + T&A?
- [ ] **Training Duration:** Standard (Manager 3 hr + Admin 6 hr) or customized?
- [ ] **Training Style:** Unifocus standard format, or PWS enhanced version?
  - *Note: Use PWS trainer reference (hospitality-contextualized) as base*

## COMMON VARIATIONS

**Large Resort (400+ rooms, 8+ departments):**
- Add: "Complex labor structure" note to scope
- Consider: Splitting training into morning/afternoon sessions by department
- Add: 30-min pre-training call with GM to understand priorities

**Union Property:**
- Add: "Confirm union contracts; Labor Rules setup may be required"
- Consider: Extra time for Work Rules configuration
- Add: Labor attorney or union rep attendance (sometimes required)

**Casino Property:**
- Add: "Complex shift patterns; 24/7 operations"
- Consider: Multiple training sessions (different shifts attend different times)
- Add: Special focus on scheduling complexity + shift rotations

**New Property (pre-opening):**
- Add: "No baseline data; we'll create model schedules together"
- Consider: Extra time on Labor Standards creation
- Add: Timeline confidence lower (new properties always shift)

**Property with Existing Unifocus (upgrade/refresh):**
- Add: "This is a refresh; some staff may have prior experience"
- Consider: Shorter training for experienced staff; focus on new features
- Add: "Bring your current setup; we'll show what's changing"
