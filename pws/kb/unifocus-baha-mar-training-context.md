## Post-Go-Live Status (as of July 2026)

### Where We Were

- Went live on schedule
- Encountered interface issues and system setting problems in the weeks following go-live
- These setbacks slowed adoption and created frustration

### Where We Are

- Most interface and settings issues have been resolved
- System is functional across the campus
- Currently in **early utilization phase** — the primary challenge is now change management, not technology
- Managers are struggling because it's new, not because it's broken — a normal and expected phase

### What Remains

**Immediate priorities:**
- Establish regular weekly labor meetings at SLS and Grand Hyatt (CTF already doing them; Rosewood has a mature rhythm)
- System administrator training (July 24–25)
- Manager training series (weeks of July 28 and August 4)
- Mobile application planning and rollout

**Change management posture:** The resistance is normal. The technology works. The goal is behavioral adoption.

## 2-Day System Administrator Training Outline

Used at Baha Mar July 24–25, 2026.

### Opening (Both Days): Survey and Listen First

Before any instruction, check in with the managers and end users in the room:
- What is frustrating them about the current state?
- What do they wish the system did better?
- What would make their week easier?

Do not pretend to know what they need. Draw it out. Use the answers to frame the training and adapt emphasis. This also surfaces issues that can be resolved during the session.

### Day 1 — Foundations

**Morning: System Orientation**
- Overview of the Unifocus ecosystem on their campus
- Key terminology and navigation
- Campus-wide system map: what's connected, what's covered, what each brand sees

**Midday: Admin Functions Deep Dive**
- User Administration (add/clone users, permission tabs: Groups-Actions-Data-Labor Structure)
- Employee Groups (bulk permission management)
- Operating Codes (creating, naming, adding jobs, granting access — and what they cannot do: no productivity measurements)
- Schedule Groups (admin creates and grants access; manager controls membership)
- Secondary jobs and Sub Only default (all secondary jobs Sub Only = Yes at start)

**Afternoon: Scheduling and Labor Cycle Fundamentals**
- Setting up schedules
- Understanding labor forecasts and trends
- Weekly labor meeting overview (admin's supporting role)

### Day 2 — Applied and Live

**Morning: Mobile Application**
- Mobile platform walkthrough
- Planning for small department pilot
- Employee User Maps (map mobile users to employee records)
- Bulk mobile invitations

**Midday: Real-Time Problem Solving**
- Reconcile Error Email (what it is, how to act on it daily)
- Hours Transfer Errors Email (same concept for T&A)
- Common issues and escalation paths
- Customizing views and dashboards
- Forecast Structure: reviewing and modifying KBIs including calculated KBIs
- Labor Standards: how to build and audit
- Planning Tab: always verify planning period is correct before doing anything

**3:00 PM: Live Labor Meeting (the capstone)**

Format: Discuss → Do → Debrief

1. **Discuss:** Admin's role in a labor meeting — prep steps, data accuracy checks, supporting department leads
2. **Do:** Two departments (pre-briefed separately) come to the room and run their actual weekly labor meeting. Admins observe and assist as appropriate. This is not a role-play — it is the real meeting.
3. **Debrief:** What did the admins see? What is their role going forward? What will they do differently next week?

**Why this format matters:** The weekly labor meeting is the most important recurring event in the Unifocus ecosystem. Admins who see it live — before they have to support it — are dramatically more confident than admins who only hear it described.

## Official Baha Mar Success Criteria

*Verbatim from the Baha Mar client success criteria document.*

### Department Managers should be able to:

- View and understand their labor standards
- Understand the Weekly Timeline and deadlines
- View, Edit and Manage Schedules
- Access and modify employee availability and other items in Employee Maintenance
- Access the help screen and the quick reference videos embedded therein
- Know how to access key reports:
  - Labor Effectiveness Report (no $)
  - Weekly Labor Summary
  - Weekly Projected Schedule Report
- Understand the information flow between Unifocus and Infor
- Understand manager functions in the mobile app
- Know how to prepare for a weekly labor meeting

### Forecasting Managers should be able to:

- Understand the source of the draft forecast
- Edit forecasts
- Know how to access key reports:
  - Rooms Forecast Report
  - Revenue Center Report

### Admins / System Champions should be able to:

- Have the same general understanding of the Department Manager functions
- Perform the following system functions:
  - Add/edit users in User Maintenance
  - Create/modify Employee Groups and Operating Codes
  - Edit Forecasts and apply Environments
  - Edit the labor structure, including:
    - Adding, moving, or putting an end date on a job
    - Managing secondary jobs
    - Resolving reconcile issues found in Employee Maintenance
    - Modifying productivity or other factors associated with the labor structure
  - Create or modify basic labor standards *(complex standards are possible over time but not an initial expectation)*
  - Create or modify revenue centers
  - Create or modify KBIs, including understanding how to build calculated KBIs
- After project transition, know who the assigned CSM is, and how to contact them, and for what
- Know how to open technical support tickets with Unifocus, and what is typically a ticket item vs. a CSM item
- Understand how to run a weekly labor meeting *(not all PS Admins will necessarily be involved in labor meetings)*
- Know how to find online help and what it includes
- Understand the current state of the knowledge base (what's available now vs. still being built)

### Across the Organization:

- Unifocus is cemented into the culture
- Unifocus reports and metrics are mentioned/discussed by leadership
- A plan is in place for new managers to learn Unifocus essentials

## Notes for Future Engagements

- **Baha Mar is a campus, not a property.** Training that works for a single-brand hotel may need adjustment for multi-brand delivery. Avoid brand-specific examples in cross-brand sessions.
- **CTF is the model.** If you need an example of what good looks like, CTF has it. Don't say this publicly — use it to calibrate expectations.
- **SLS and Grand Hyatt are at the beginning.** Patience and repetition. Change management, not technology.
- **Rosewood is the proof point.** If anyone doubts this works, point to Rosewood (quietly).
- **The weekly labor meeting is the anchor.** Once it becomes a habit, the rest follows. Everything else is setup for that meeting.
