**Context:** Pete is talking with Ralph Varble on 8/28/26 (Ralph's last Friday before his 8/31/26 departure from Unifocus) to capture institutional knowledge before he leaves — starting with the Rockbridge/MakeReady relationship. ## Tom Healy — Who He Is

**Public role:** President and COO of **Rockbridge Hospitality** and its sister company **Bridgepoint Hospitality**, based in Columbus, Ohio. Appointed to that role around **April 2022**, per multiple hospitality trade press announcements. Spelling confirmed as "Healy" (no "e" before the "y"), consistent both internally and publicly.

**Internal history — long-standing, high-trust Unifocus relationship:** Tom Healy has been a longtime contact between Unifocus (Ralph Varble, Ken Heymann, Steve Carrell, and Pete) and Rockbridge going back years.

- **November 2023 thread** (re: a missing signed contract for The Lodge at Sonoma) — Ralph described how work with Rockbridge historically got started on a handshake: *"Tom would call Ken and say 'go' and we went... Tom knew we would go and Ken knew Tom would ensure we got paid."* Ken Heymann (former Unifocus exec) reportedly used a stripped-down "short form" agreement specifically with trusted contacts like Tom.
- **July 2023** — Ken Heymann noted Mike Ditterline had left and "now works with Tom Healy over at Rockbridge." Ditterline currently shows up in Rockbridge's asset-management contact lists as **Asset Manager for several MakeReady properties** (Hotel Derek, The Adolphus, The Junto, Penn Stater) — likely the team Tom oversees or works alongside.
- Tom himself doesn't appear as a line contact in the current Rockbridge asset-list spreadsheet (which lists asset managers like Nate Sporbert, Michael Damitio, Jason Deville, Carly Thorp — all @rockbridgeholdings.com) — consistent with him sitting above the day-to-day asset-manager layer as President/COO, not an operational point of contact.

## Rockbridge — Scope of the Relationship

**Much bigger than just MakeReady/Pete's 3 properties.** Rockbridge is a large, active Unifocus client relationship — SharePoint holds hundreds of related documents (property analysis decks, asset lists, change orders, standards-test sign-offs) spanning brands under the Rockbridge umbrella: **Makeready, HVMG, Aimbridge, Davidson, HRI Hospitality, Pinnacle, Scholar**, and others.

**No direct contact info found** for Tom Healy himself (no email/phone in mailbox or SharePoint). If Pete needs to reach him directly, **Nate Sporbert** or **Michael Ditterline** (both current Rockbridge contacts already in Pete's files) would likely connect him.
