## Tuesday 9/16/26 — Pete's Own Diagnosis: Universal Sub-Only Import Defect (Corrects the 9/14 Framing)

**This corrects, not just extends, the 9/14 update above.** Nguon's 9/14 explanation for Allegory Bar ("all employees flagged Sub Only = Yes except one role") was accurate as far as it went, but Pete has now confirmed the actual scope is **property-wide, not outlet-specific**: on initial import, **every primary/home job was set Sub Only = Yes, and every secondary job was set Sub Only = No — backwards from correct.** Since most employees hold only their one primary job, this single inverted flag excludes the majority of the hourly workforce from auto-scheduling, regardless of Assignments, KBI volume, or any of the other explanations investigated so far.

**Concrete confirmed example (screenshot, Employee Maintenance > Jobs tab):** Villar, Mavire C (FDX000098), Room Attendant at The Moran —
| Job | Rate | Sub-Only |
|---|---|---|
| 01. Rooms Division\Housekeeping\**Room Attendant** (home job, actual job) | $13.39/hr | **Yes** |
| 01. Rooms Division\Housekeeping\Houseperson (not her real job) | $1.00/hr | No |
| 01. Rooms Division\Laundry\Attendant Laundry (not her real job) | $1.00/hr | No |

Her real job is flagged Sub Only = Yes (excluding her from auto-scheduling in it), while two placeholder-rate jobs she doesn't actually work are flagged No. This is the same inverted pattern across the property, not a per-outlet quirk.

**The 9/11 fill-rate "improvement" (7% → 39%) is not real progress and must be corrected in the client-facing letter:** that gain came entirely from newly created contract-labor placeholder employees, not from any fix to the underlying Sub-Only defect. Actual regular-employee auto-scheduling is exactly as broken now as it was on 8/31 — the placeholders simply added filled shifts on top of the same broken base.

**Status reverted 9/16/26: 🟠 At Risk | Since Previous Update: Unchanged** (not the 🟡 Caution/Improved call made earlier the same day based on the incomplete 9/14 read — corrected before anything was sent to the property or Alain).

**Real fix required:** a bulk correction reversing the Sub-Only flag — primary/home jobs to No, secondary jobs to Yes — across the property's employee base, not case-by-case fixes to individual jobs (Laundry Attendant, Allegory Bar) as previously scoped.
