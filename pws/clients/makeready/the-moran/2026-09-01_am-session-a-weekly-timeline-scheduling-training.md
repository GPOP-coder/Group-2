## 4. Revised Weekly Forecast-to-Schedule Cycle (Taught as the Live Plan)

Pete taught the cycle worked out with Monica the night before as the property's actual go-forward rhythm:
- **Thursday:** Open new planning period (automatic, early AM). Rooms forecast + banquet forecast must be entered by **Thursday 4:00 PM**.
- **Thursday 4:00 PM:** Generate Revenue Center Forecast runs automatically.
- **Thursday 4:00 PM – Friday ~12:00 PM:** F&B revenue-center forecast edit window.
- **Friday ~12:00 PM (noon):** Generate Projected Hours, then Generate Schedules — this is the point at which managers' draft schedules become editable. Pete: *"Pretend, think about, imagine that your schedule is going to get erased before that point."*
- **Friday afternoon → Wednesday:** Managers build/edit schedules.
- **Wednesday afternoon:** Weekly labor meeting (LLG-equivalent) — come with schedules finished plus last-week/next-week readiness reports.
- **End of day Wednesday:** Schedules published.
- Pete flagged that Friday noon is also when *he* leaves the property ("I'm gonna be on an airplane by then") — a direct link to why UNIFOCUS-261575 needs to be resolved and validated before he departs.

## 7. Scheduler Screen Mechanics (Taught Without a Live Generated Schedule)

- **Filter mechanics — explicit callback to the "40% of confusion" footgun** flagged during the 8/31 session: the **Schedule Group** filter and the **By Job** filter are independent and combine with AND logic. Pete demonstrated this directly and told managers plainly not to use Schedule Groups yet — filter by division/department/job instead, since schedule groups start empty and require separate permissioning.
- **Assignments**, revisited with a live example: cook and room attendant jobs have sub-level "assignments" (e.g., Daylight Saving vs. Standard Time start-time variants). Pete tried to manually drag a cook shift to an employee and hit an **assignment violation** — the employee's Employee Maintenance record hadn't been given that assignment yet, so the shift couldn't be assigned until that's corrected.
- **Drag/drop constraints demonstrated live:** can't drop a shift on a day other than the shift's own day; can't drop a job's shift onto an employee who lacks that job; can't double-book a shift onto someone already scheduled without an explicit override.
- **Visual cues:** a shift added manually by a manager shows a small coffee-cup icon (distinguishing "manager-edited" from system-generated); each employee has a horizontal "thermometer" bar showing hours filling up toward their target (40 hrs full-time, or a lower part-time threshold) so managers can see at a glance when adding another shift would trigger overtime; overtime is never automatic — managers must add it deliberately.
- **"The numbers at the bottom"** — the daily/weekly shift-count and hours summary panel — is what everyone outside the manager's own view actually looks at (Pete: labor meeting participants care about "are we ready, are we covered," not who specifically is on the schedule).
- Two distinct bartender job titles exist on the system (used across Allegory and TBR outlets) with the same display name, causing confusion about who's eligible for which shift — flagged as needing cleanup (see §12).
- **Print schedule** feature exists but pulls a different layout/setting set than the on-screen editor; Pete deferred detailed print-formatting help to later in the week once real schedules are entered, offering to match the old Excel-based printed format managers are used to.
- **Manage Schedules** screen (complete → approve → publish workflow, tied to the mobile app) was shown again briefly as a preview — not in active use this week or possibly this year; employees won't see anything on the mobile app until a schedule is published.

## 8. ADP Integration & Data Flow

- Schedule data flows **one direction only**, Unifocus → ADP, once per day (not real-time) — managers should never enter schedules directly in ADP.
- The Export Schedules screen displays timestamps in **GMT, not local time** — flagged as the one screen where that's true, to avoid confusion (e.g., a timestamp reading "1:02 PM" when the local time was actually 9:48 AM).
- Employee roster and actual hours import from ADP into Unifocus daily, picking up not just yesterday's punches but a rolling **14-day window** of changes — addressing a known gap in the prior LLG system, which reportedly only picked up a single day.

## 9. Contract Labor Placeholders

- Housekeeping runs as much as **~80% contract labor** on peak days (Valeria: 15–16 room attendants needed for a full turn on the busiest days, e.g., an approaching storm; only 3 are currently set up as real, named employees in the system).
- Fix: create anonymous **placeholder employees** per job (e.g., "Room Attendant 1," "Room Attendant 2" ... up to however many are needed), fed automatically from **Work Records** hours (contract labor timekeeping), so shift counts and labor reports reflect true headcount without needing to name individual agency workers. Placeholders can either auto-fill after regular full-time employees are scheduled, or be filled manually — approach still to be decided.
- Contract labor hours previously had to be manually uploaded into the old system via a **Citrix**-hosted LLG session; this is no longer necessary — anything landing in Work Records now flows in automatically, as long as it's checked regularly (the same 14-day window as ADP punches).
