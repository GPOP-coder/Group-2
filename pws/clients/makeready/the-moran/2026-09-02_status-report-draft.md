## Detail — Work Rules, Interfaces, Standards

**Work Rules**

No further work-rule issues surfaced this week.

**Interfaces**
**ADP** — one-direction schedule export to ADP once daily (not real-time); employee/actuals import from ADP daily on a rolling 14-day window, which also means a corrected ADP record takes roughly 1–2 days to fully propagate. Multiple live data-quality issues found and referred to the property/HR for correction in ADP (not overridden in Unifocus): three Bell staff importing under Valet's department code; a bartender working both Allegory and TBR/Boardroom coded as one generic job with no outlet distinction; a Front Desk employee missing his second (Night Auditor) job code entirely, raising a possible unpaid shift-differential concern — confirmed live again 9/3/26; two additional Front Desk staff coded as Valet Parking rather than Bell; **new 9/3/26** — several Allegory/Café Moran staff who work banquets as a secondary job are not double-coded as banquet servers in ADP, so they don't appear when a manager filters for that job. A terminated Engineering employee also remained active/schedulable in Unifocus for roughly a day due to normal ADP termination-sync timing — expected behavior, not a defect, but a reminder to also mark terminated staff unavailable in Employee Maintenance in the interim. **New 9/3/26:** MakeReady corporate payroll (Brandy Becker) has independently compiled a list of ADP double-coded employees not populating correctly in Unifocus — a potential batch-fix resource for several of the above at once; not yet acted on. **New 9/3/26:** Export Schedules was found not running on its own automated schedule as expected; needs its own support ticket. **Delphi** — the banquet forecast is still entered manually by the Director of Finance rather than by its intended owner (Sales); the daily rooms+banquet export from Delphi is also still a manual, once-a-day action by Sales, since Delphi cannot auto-send to non-client addresses. The intended automation (a scheduled Delphi report → Sales → forwarded to PMSfiles@unifocus.com, then mapped by Unifocus data) is designed but not yet implemented. Separately, banquet BEOs are not being consistently closed out in Delphi by their owners — when that happens, the banquet labor standard gets zero credit for labor actually worked, which shows up as an unexplained overage at the labor meeting. **ProfitSword** — Allegory's beverage revenue (beer/wine/liquor) is confirmed entirely absent from the raw ProfitSword export, a ProfitSword/Actabl-side data issue rather than a Unifocus mapping problem; the property has already escalated directly to ProfitSword/Actabl support. This matters because beverage revenue (not covers) is the number intended to drive the bartender labor standard. **Separate finding, 9/3/26:** the Allegory bartender labor standard itself is showing zero generated standard hours despite beverage revenue being present in the forecast — a possible standard/KBI-mapping issue, not yet diagnosed. **Generate Schedules (UNIFOCUS-261575)** — see below.

**Labor Standards**
Room Attendant is built on a minutes-per-unit model (30 min/departure, 18 min/stayover at 92% of stayover count to account for Do Not Disturb declines) plus a separate "staff minutes" KBI adding 15 minutes per stand-up-meeting attendee. Housekeeping's loaded productivity goal (0.88, inherited from a prior management company and not trusted by current Finance leadership) does not reconcile with a live productivity-report reading of 6.17 or with the property's recalled historical target from a prior era (1.45) — worth investigating once more data accumulates, not resolved this week. The outlet (a la carte) cook standard has a covers-volume trigger that Pete and a department manager believe may be mislabeled (reading "AM" where it should read "PM" covers) — **confirmed live again 9/3/26; Pete attempted to correct it live and could not, hitting an unexplained edit-permission block** (possibly related to the broader system issues behind 261575) — still not corrected, pending resolution of that block. The banquet cook/chef standard runs on a different, minutes-accumulate-per-cover model that gives zero labor credit for any banquet whose BEO was never closed out or never had an actual entered — a real, explainable source of standard-vs-actual variance once the Delphi closeout gap above is understood. Chef Attendant, Carving/Action Station, and extra-bartender needs are all manual input KBIs (Unifocus reads only Event Type and Booking Type off a BEO, not free text or dollar amounts) — someone has to key these in on both forecast and actuals for that portion of the standard to generate any shifts at all; no single owner has been assigned yet; **confirmed 9/3/26 that the Action Station KBI is also capped at generating one shift regardless of the number entered** — a separate limitation Pete flagged he can't fix before departure. Housekeeping is running an estimated ~80% contract labor on peak days against only 3 real named employees currently in the system; contract-labor placeholder employees (an estimated 10–15 for Room Attendant, 1 for Bell) are agreed in concept but not yet built — naming convention still undecided.

**Labor Structure**
Only non-exempt employees are currently being scheduled in Unifocus; salaried staff (e.g., the Culinary sous chef) are not scheduled under the current setup. The Sub Only flag (which prevents the auto-scheduler from filling a secondary job for an employee) was pre-populated correctly for existing secondary-job employees at go-live, but is not automatic going forward — it's a manager responsibility for any newly cross-trained or transferred employee, and at least one Front Desk case required manual correction this week. Two distinct bartender job codes (Allegory and TBR/Boardroom) currently display under the same generic "Bartender" title, creating ambiguity about shift eligibility — flagged for ADP-side cleanup, not yet done. New hires start with no Schedule Group assigned by default and will not appear in schedule-group-filtered views until a manager sets one manually. **New 9/3/26:** a live attempt to remove an incorrect secondary Steward job assignment from a Culinary employee also failed with the same unexplained edit-permission block noted above under Labor Standards — worth tracking as a possible symptom cluster.
