## 1 · Training Session Types

We run two sessions. Attendees join the one that matches their role.

| Session | Who Attends | What It Covers | Length | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Managers | Managers responsible for Planning and scheduling | Principles of Labor Management, LMS Timeline, Employee Maintenance, Planning/Scheduling, Reports, Labor Dashboard | 3 hrs | 20 ppl |
| Admins | Anyone designated to administer the Labor Management System | Revenue Centers, Key Business Indicators, Labor/Global Environments, Planning, Labor Structure, Labor Standards, Work Rules, Emp Set-up, Weekly Labor Management Meeting, Security Configuration | 6 hrs | 5 ppl |

*Note: Managers (majority of users) must attend one Manager session; Admins (GM, Finance, HR) must attend both Manager and Admin sessions.*
