- Same one-page weekly cycle walked through with all prior groups (8/31, 9/1): planning period opens automatically Thursday morning; rooms + banquet forecast due by 4:00 PM Thursday; Generate Revenue Center Forecast fires automatically at 4:00 PM; F&B revenue-center edit window runs Thursday 4:00 PM through Friday noon; Generate Projected Hours + Generate Schedules fire automatically Friday ~noon; managers edit/build schedules Friday afternoon through Wednesday; Wednesday afternoon labor meeting; schedules published end of day Wednesday.

- Pete reiterated the **guest-count vs. room-count** framing (a pageant/dance-competition group can run several guests per room) and that banquet forecasting is intentionally *not* reading BEO line items — Unifocus only sees the aggregate KBI numbers someone enters.

## 2. "It Makes Shifts, Then It Fills Them" — Reinforced with the Kitchen Analogy

- Pete used a live back-and-forth with Danilo and Christian to reinforce that culinary/banquet labor happens ahead of the event date (prep 24–48 hours out), unlike LG's single-day productivity math which assumed same-day labor. Christian and Danilo both independently confirmed this matches their real workflow and both expressed frustration with LG's rigid day-by-day accounting for exactly this reason.
- Christian raised whether the system could "bank hours" the way LG nominally allowed — Pete's answer: no explicit banked-hours ledger exists, but the same effect is achieved by looking at variance over a week (or month) rather than a single day — "the banking comes in the altitude."
- Reiterated the two labor-meeting questions ("How did we do last week? Are we ready for next week?") and that the schedule functions like a labor purchase order the labor meeting approves.
