## Day 2 — July 15: Labor Standards

Hands-on walkthrough on JW Marriott Houston (Rooms, then F&B/Banquets). Core concepts delivered: Shift/Schedule/Standard vocabulary, Pitcher & Glasses model, rounding threshold, fixed vs. KBI-driven standards, minutes-per-unit mechanics (two-pass distribution, short shift expectation), negative-hours netting. Real broken configuration found and fixed live: JW Houston Club Lounge Breakfast Attendant (inconsistent shift record vs. naming convention — corrected to two clean shift records).

Banquet philosophy stated explicitly and repeatedly: banquet standards are inherently estimate-based, not precise — the system has no BEO visibility, only daily totals. Practical consequence: evaluate banquet culinary/stewarding/houseperson over the week, not day-by-day, since standard hours land on the event date while actual labor spans the days before.

**Lead to follow up:** Banquet Bartender standard at JW Houston may be mapped to Dinner only (missing Receptions). Needs verification with the property or Elliott Welburn before treating as confirmed — session was interrupted by a platform outage before verification was complete.
