## Banquet Captain/Bartender KBI Clarification (portfolio-wide, surfaced via La Paloma)

- Bartender count in banquets is driven entirely by a manual **input KBI** — a manager types the number in from the BEO's notes field. **There is no interfaceable KBI for bartender count** — no EMS field carries it structurally; it only lives in BEO free-text notes describing the bar setup. The input number then generates the corresponding shifts for the scheduling manager.
- Captain uses the same manual-input mechanism as bartender, per John Grech's clarification (confirmed against Pete's own call transcript): the input field is a **headcount** ("number of people"), not hours and not covers. The recurring property-side mistake John had previously caught was La Paloma entering the number of **hours** needed instead of the number of **bodies** — e.g. typing "10" meaning 10 hours of captain coverage, when the standard treats the input as bodies × a fixed hours-per-body figure (so entering "2" for 2 people, not "16" for the resulting hours). **La Paloma did not repeat this mistake on this week's forecast** — confirmed fixed.
- Alain and Taylor still planned to grab 10–15 minutes to walk the planning-screen setup together, mainly to confirm this mechanism doesn't collide with anything covers-driven elsewhere on the same standard.
- **Cross-reference:** this is the same underlying gap already tracked at Marriott Del Mar (no bartender job/standard exists at all there — see Del Mar kb.md open items) and at Westin La Paloma (La Luz Lounge beverage-revenue standard) and now surfaced again at Marriott Del Mar's own Thursday manager training the same day (bar schedule still occupancy-based, zero beverage revenue flowing). **This looks like a genuine portfolio-wide pattern, not a series of unrelated property issues** — worth eventually treating as one methodology question across all of HMAlpha rather than fixing property-by-property.
