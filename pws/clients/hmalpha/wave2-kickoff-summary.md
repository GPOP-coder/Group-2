## Key Points for HM Alpha Wave 2 Properties

*(Consolidated from Session 4 of 5; will be refined/corrected as remaining sessions are added — later sessions may surface Q&A specific to individual properties.)*

**Timeline:** One week on-site consultant configuration visit → gap week for reconciliation → one week on-site training → go-live.

**Consultant scope:** Clone/adapt Wave 1 pilot configurations (except one pilot property, not yet specified which). Build labor standards, cleaning minutes, tasks, and special exceptions live during configuration.

**Integrations:** Paychex (payroll/timekeeping), ProfitSword (volume/transaction data), event system per brand flag (CI/TY, Envision, or Delphi — see [kb.md](kb.md) brand/EMS table) for banquet granularity, WorkRecords (contract labor). Approved PTO must be in Paychex to show in Unifocus.

**Hotel responsibilities:** Deliver pre-work (restaurant names, meal periods, hours, seat counts) by deadline. Populate scheduling grids with staff names/availability before the on-site visit. Prioritize early-day appointments during the implementation week. Provide a back-of-house tour. Keep employee maintenance (hour caps, availability, preferences) current to reduce manual schedule edits.

**Training:** Manager training ~2–2.5 hours. Admin/property champion training is longer, hands-on, laptops required, done on the live (not sandbox) system.

**Reporting demo:** Month-long trend graph at property level with 4 colored lines — standard (navy blue, actual volume × standard), actual worked (green), projected (pink, forecasted volume × standard), and scheduled/recommended (color TBD). Used to teach two things: (1) actual-vs-standard tightness = execution discipline win; (2) projected-vs-standard gap = forecast accuracy issue. Property-level view can mask offsetting department-level errors — drill-down to division/department/job available in live system. See [generic kickoff guide](../../kb/unifocus-implementation-kickoff-guide.md#trend-graph-demo) for full detail.
