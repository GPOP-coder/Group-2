**⚠️ 8/21/26, 2:26pm — Taylor Walton email to the HM Alpha consultant team (Akram Chahin, Steve Carrell, John Grech, Pete, TAPS Interfaces, Alain Derderian; cc Ralph Varble), subject "HM Alpha Schedule Export Update" — corrects/refines the above, real live incident, real workaround (not just "wait a week"):**
- **Confirmed root mechanism:** the export interface (1) checks if any UF schedules are Published, (2) if yes, pulls and deletes **all** Paychex schedules for Today+14 days — **no filter limiting the delete to only the Published date range** — then (3) applies only Published UF shifts for that same Today+14 window back to Paychex.
- **DoubleTree Charlotte hit this live 8/20/26:** clicked Publish on their first schedule for Planning Period Aug 22–28. Because Aug 20–21 were still blank/unpublished in UF (training hadn't reached those days yet), the delete step wiped Paychex's real Aug 20–21 schedules with nothing in UF to reapply — total loss for those two days. Taylor had the team manually re-enter Aug 21 directly into UF (not Paychex, since Paychex entries would just get deleted again on the next export cycle).
- **The real fix Taylor is instructing all HM Alpha consultants to use going forward:** when a property is ready to Publish for the first time, don't just publish the upcoming Planning Period — **also enter and Publish the remaining days of the current week between today and the Planning Period's start date.** Example given: training ends Wednesday, team wants to publish next Saturday's planning week — instruct them to also enter/publish Thursday–Friday (today through the gap) so the delete-and-reapply cycle has real shifts to restore for those days too. Only forward-looking days matter (export doesn't touch the past), so this is a one-time, first-publish-only exposure — normal weeks afterward have no gap since the prior week is already published.
