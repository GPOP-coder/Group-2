2. **Terminated employee 26300471** — moved to inactive 9/2/26, but is missing actual-hours display for worked shifts from **Aug 19–Sept 1, 2026**. Escalated by Taylor to the Unifocus Interface team; resolution pending. Correct behavior: worked shifts (actual hours) should interface from Paychex for all shifts up through the termination date, for every employee — this one didn't.
3. **Salaried managers' shifts were getting an unwanted 30-min unpaid-lunch adjustment.** Fixed going forward: salaried jobs are now excluded from that adjustment on all newly generated shifts.
4. **Paychex/Unifocus scheduled-hours display mismatch, explained (not a bug):** Paychex calculates scheduled hours including the unpaid lunch break baked into the shift, so an employee scheduled for 40 hours over 5 shifts in Unifocus shows as 42.5 scheduled hours in Paychex. No pay impact — purely a display difference. Employees should be told their Paychex-displayed scheduled hours include the unpaid 30-min lunch for qualifying shifts, so the discrepancy doesn't cause confusion.
