## 1. Standard Driver Confirmed — Beverage Revenue, Not Covers

- La Lu's Lounge (bar) **servers/cocktail servers are staffed off of beverage revenue, not covers** — explicitly reasoned through live: *"they don't make food, they make drinks... we don't have covers for drinks. We do have beverage revenue."*
- **Working assumption behind the model:** average drink price is treated as roughly consistent day to day (a mix of $5 and $10 items blending out), so a **dollar-revenue threshold is a reasonable proxy for drink volume/staffing need**, even without a true per-drink count.
- **Bartenders were confirmed to run on a different logic than servers** — the bartender is a fixed presence regardless of volume (see below), while the server/cocktail-server role scales with revenue thresholds.

## 2. Server/Cocktail Server Thresholds (Beverage Revenue, Combined With Swing Suite Revenue)

- **Under $2,000/day combined beverage revenue (La Lu's + Swing Suite): no server at all** — the bartender alone handles service.
- **$2,000–$3,000: one 6-hour server shift.**
- **At $3,000: that same shift extends to a full 8-hour shift.**
- **At $3,750: a second shift is added** (a 5pm start on weekdays).
- **At $3,750 on weekends specifically: the added shift's start time shifts later** — same total shift count, just a later start (framed as "the shift gets longer," meaning later closing, not more shifts) to match later weekend guest patterns.
- **At $4,000: that weekend shift extends further** (later still).
- Swing Suite revenue is explicitly folded into this same beverage-revenue total even though it's technically a separate revenue center — confirmed as intentional, and confirmed as a small dollar contributor overall, not a major driver.

## 3. Bartender Coverage — Fixed, Not Revenue-Scaled

- **One bartender is scheduled every single day at 11am, no matter what** — a fixed opener, independent of any revenue threshold.
- **A second bartender shift starts at 5pm every day, no matter what** — also fixed.
- **On Friday and Saturday specifically, that 5pm start moves to 6pm instead** — attributed to the property being open later and guests lingering longer on weekend nights, so shifting the second bartender's start an hour later avoids running into overtime while still covering the later close (shift runs to approximately 1:30am on those nights).
- **A weekend "mid" shift is added at $5,000/day beverage revenue** — this is the one point in the model where the bartender side does scale with revenue, layered on top of the two fixed opener/closer shifts.
