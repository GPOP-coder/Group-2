# Westin La Paloma — Office Hours (Thursday AM), Part 2: Paychex Sync & Publishing Mechanics

**Date:** August 20, 2026, AM
**Attending:** Spa department manager/lead (question originated here); general audience as other managers cycled through office hours; Pete Castellano.
**Source:** Raw voice-memo/session transcript, rough auto-transcription with crosstalk, not cleaned verbatim; structured below.
**Note on scope:** This re-covers and sharpens the Paychex sync cadence already taught in [Day 1 §9](2026-08-17_admin-training-day1-monday.md#9-manage-schedules--complete--approve--publish-workflow) and reconfirmed in [Tuesday PM §7](2026-08-18_manager-training-tuesday-pm.md#7-manage-schedules--completeapprovepublish-still-evolving) — this file focuses on the **direction of sync (schedule → Paychex) and the clock-lock risk**, which is new detail not previously captured this precisely.

## 1. Draft Cadence Reconfirmed

- **The schedule drafts Friday at 3:30pm** — whatever is sitting in Unifocus at that moment becomes the starting point for the upcoming published week.
- From that point forward, **Unifocus pushes schedule data to Paychex roughly twice a day** (once around end-of-day, possibly an afternoon pull as well — exact second time uncertain, tentatively tied to the Wednesday labor-meeting cycle: "Wednesday after our labor meetings, will it pull again?").

## 2. ⚠️ Only Published Schedules Sync to Paychex

- **Critical mechanic: unpublished schedules do NOT get sent to Paychex.** Pete was visibly uncertain in the moment but landed on this as the operating assumption — consistent with not wanting employees to see (or clock in against) a schedule that hasn't been finalized.
- This directly explains why some departments weren't yet seeing anything populate in Paychex — **publishing had not happened yet** for those schedules.

## 3. Sync Direction Is Forward-Only

- **The Unifocus → Paychex push only sends today-forward (or tomorrow-forward), never backward.** Changing something in Unifocus for a past date will not retroactively update Paychex.
- Practical implication for same-day changes: **if a change is needed for today** (e.g., a provider who was supposed to start at 9am is now coming in at noon), making that edit in Unifocus is unreliable for getting it into Paychex in time — **make same-day changes directly in Paychex instead.**
- **Rule of thumb given directly:** edits for **today** → do them in Paychex. Edits for **tomorrow and beyond** → do them in Unifocus, since those will sync before the day arrives.

## 4. Employee-Facing Visibility — Paychex App Already Shows Schedules

- Employees can already see their schedules (and presumably balances) via the **Paychex mobile app** today, independent of whether Unifocus training/rollout is complete for their department.
- **This creates a real risk during the transition period:** if Unifocus schedules aren't accurate yet, employees checking Paychex could see (and rely on) wrong information. Pete's direct statement to the room: **"we don't want to send them the wrong schedule."**
- Long-term intent (not yet fully communicated to staff): employees should eventually look to **Unifocus (via its own app) for schedules**, and to **Paychex only for pay, time-off requests, and HR-related items** — but **"that dividing line is still a little fuzzy"** since Paychex is still the thing employees check today.

## 5. ⚠️ Open Risk — Clock Lockout Once Paychex Schedules Start Reflecting Unifocus

- Once schedules start actively flowing from Unifocus into Paychex (rather than being manually maintained there), and **if Paychex's clock-in lockout is active** (an employee can't clock in without a matching Paychex schedule row — the same issue flagged in [Day 1](2026-08-17_admin-training-day1-monday.md)), there's a real risk of **overriding whatever a manager already typed directly into Paychex** for that day.
- **Explicitly unresolved at the time of this session:** whether **Jeanette** (HR) has decided to undo/disable that Paychex clock-lock. Pete's direct assessment: **"you are heading for a brick wall right now"** if that decision isn't made before schedules start actively pushing into Paychex at scale.
- **Action flagged as needing same-day resolution:** get with Jeanette to resolve the clock-lock question before this becomes an operational problem — property teams need to understand that **Unifocus is becoming the single source of truth for schedules**, and stop treating Paychex schedule edits as durable once the sync is live.

## 6. Practical Guidance Given to the Spa Manager

- **If you already know a change applies for a future date, put it in Unifocus** — it'll carry over cleanly.
- **If it's an in-the-moment, same-day fix just to get someone clocked in, do it in Paychex directly** — don't rely on Unifocus to catch up in time.
- Bottom line stated plainly: **"We're not gonna try to take over your time [and attendance system]... The only thing that we are doing is we're informing the schedule in Paychex."**
