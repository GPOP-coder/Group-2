- These numbers pre-date a since-abandoned attempt (originated by "C" — likely Steve or a prior consultant) to build a full revenue-center forecast for spa treatments treated like meal periods (breakfast/lunch/dinner-style forecasting). **That KBI-based forecast structure is built but shut off/unused** — not relevant to how spa actually runs today.

## 3. Permanent Scheduling — Deliberately Overriding the Standard for Providers

- The spa department was advised to use **Permanent** status (not Regular, not Variable) for therapist/technician availability, and to make it match **Paychex-style/book-for-time** hours purely so providers can clock in without extra manual work — not because the department needs the auto-scheduler's help filling shifts.
- **Key mechanic: Permanent scheduling ignores the labor standard entirely.** Regular status will stop generating shifts once the standard's hour cap (e.g., 61 hours/day) is reached; Permanent keeps going regardless. Pete's explicit caveat: **"I wouldn't tell that to hardly anybody else, but you're putting people on the schedule for a different reason"** — this is a deliberate, spa-specific exception, not general guidance.
- Rationale: spa needs to schedule **more providers than the 61-hour standard would generate** in order to actually cover walk-in/booked demand — the standard number is treated as a rough budget guide, not an operating ceiling, for this department specifically.

## 4. Availability Setup — Example Walkthrough (Raquel)

- Walked through Administration → Employee Maintenance → filter by job (Spa → Technician) to set availability, since **Assignment cannot be filtered on directly** for this purpose.
- **Preferred Time Off is a soft signal, not a real constraint** — Pete recommended not using it ("preferred, by the way, doesn't do anything... it's a good solution for spa. It's permanent scheduling.").
- For an all-day close (e.g., someone has the whole day off), block the entire day rather than trying to represent a partial-day preference — even for a property whose actual hours of operation are 9–5, blocking the full day is simpler and functionally equivalent.
- Recommended pattern reinforced for spa: **set Required Off correctly for days off, then rely on Permanent + wide availability to cover working days** — this is the same "know your standard, then match employee maintenance to it" principle taught elsewhere in this session (see [Part 5 — Reconcile Employees](2026-08-20_office-hours-am-reconcile-employees-job-code-mismatches.md)) but deliberately relaxed for spa via Permanent.
