## Communication Flow

### Consultant ↔ Property Communication
- Consultants communicate **directly** with properties for:
  - Scheduling adjustments (timing, dates)
  - Manager/GM vacation conflicts
  - Training session moves or rescheduling
- **HM Alpha is CC'd** on all consultant-to-property communications
- **Benefit to HM Alpha:** Reduces operational burden; consultant handles property-level details directly

### Status Updates
- Provided at **end of each visit** (not daily)
- Format: Status letter/recap note
- Sent to HM Alpha (cc'd stakeholders)

---

## Pre-Coordination Process

**Nicole Mendez's workflow (before consultant arrival):**
1. Pre-coordinate with hotels on key personnel PTO/time off
2. Verify no conflicts with "must-have" attendees
3. Create "sandstone" (flexible but pre-vetted) timeline
4. Confirm with consultant and property
5. Consultant retains right to make week-of adjustments if needed

**Rationale:** Respect for consultant time and property operations; reduce last-minute surprises

---

## Status Letter Expectations

**Content required:**
- What was accomplished during the visit
- What is pending (next visit or follow-up items)
- Any blockers or issues

**Timing:** End of each training visit/milestone
