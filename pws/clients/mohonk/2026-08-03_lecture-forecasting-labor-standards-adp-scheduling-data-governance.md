### System Architecture: KBIs, Forecasting Methods, and Environments
**Conclusion:** KBIs operate as input, calculated, or revenue-center types. Revenue center forecasting uses Regression or TES on the last 13 weeks, with manual edits permitted. "Environments" isolate anomalies but are advanced/manual-intensive.
- TES weights recent weeks but can't predict sharp shifts; Regression handles capture ratios but can fail when variable relationships degrade
- Environments require diligence tagging both future and past analogous dates — not currently recommended for routine use
- Two independent variables drive F&B forecasts: available guests (rooms forecast adjusted for group banquets) and banquet forecast (moving from manual to Infor SC)
