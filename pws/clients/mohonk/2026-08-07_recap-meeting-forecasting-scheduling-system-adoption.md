## AI Meeting Notes (Plaud summary, in full)

### I. Forecasting and Scheduling Process Improvement
- **Current process:** everything moves forward Sunday night, forecasting Monday. Rooms forecast is automated via DataVision; banquets forecast is manually entered by James, with automation planned.
- **Proposed timeline shift:** revenue forecasts generated by Monday 12:00 PM, edits complete by Tuesday noon, managers build schedules through Thursday noon.
- **Labor meeting/publication:** moves from Wednesday to Thursday (2:00–3:00 PM); schedules finalized and posted Thursday evening — a full day earlier than current practice.
- **Risk:** compressing the timeline leaves less margin for error if automated data feeds run late.

### II. System Adoption and Change Management
- **Single system mandate:** consolidate scheduling into Unifocus only, eliminate paper/Excel/other systems — proposed as a "friendly mandate": all schedules must be generated and printed from Unifocus.
- **Managerial practices:** stop copying last week's schedule as a starting point; originate schedules in the system. Pre-entered ("regular") shifts aren't overwritten by generation. Core message: focus on "the numbers at the bottom" (projected vs. scheduled).
- **Department-specific training:** 1–2 hr sessions planned for Recreation, Spa, Properties, Housekeeping — practical/task-focused (e.g., printing schedules).
- **Mobile app adoption:** push app to employees first, let them pressure managers into using it as the single live source.

### III. Department-Specific Considerations
- **Banquets:** BEOs must be closed out in Infor for accurate labor forecasting; labor allocates to the event day even if setup/teardown spans other days.
- **Housekeeping:** daily numbers can look off due to weekend catch-up; focus on total weekly need, which is accurate over 7 days.
- **Spa:** needs a scheduler adoption plan — goal is coverage/availability (understaffing risks lost revenue), not minute labor optimization. Start with Spa reception. Standards should range from simple (total treatments vs. hours) to detailed (by service type/duration).
- **Overscheduling strategy:** James's department intentionally overschedules via "on-call" status, which shows as overscheduled in the system — a deliberate flexibility tactic, not suitable for every department.

### IV. Data Integration, Reporting, and Labor Analysis
- **Time-off requests:** approved in Unifocus by Monday → honored in the following week's generated schedule. Late requests need manual manager action.
- **ADP "punch to schedule":** hours currently default to an employee's ADP "home job" instead of where actually worked (e.g., culinary interns). Fix requires adding secondary jobs in ADP + automating the Unifocus→ADP schedule export (**~$4,000 estimated**).
- **Labor analytics:** a combined ADP + schedule report was highlighted as valuable for drilling into scheduled vs. actual hours per employee — surfaces trends like chronic OT or six-day weeks.
- **Daily punch edits:** need to reinforce a culture of daily time-punch editing; a daily exception/unapproved-time report was suggested.

### V. Role Design and Meeting Reflections
