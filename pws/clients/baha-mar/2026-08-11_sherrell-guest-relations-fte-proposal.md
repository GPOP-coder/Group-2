## Scope

224-room resort. New merged Guest Relations function covers two distinct sub-functions:

1. **Guest Experience Team** — VIP/repeat guest recognition, pre-arrival outreach, transportation arrangements, restaurant/spa reservations, special occasion coordination, amenity/preference management, arrival experience planning, in-house guest engagement and problem resolution.
2. **PBX / Call Center** — 24-hour phone coverage, internal/external calls, guest request dispatching, emergency communications, wake-up calls, service tracking, service recovery routing, coordination with Front Office, Housekeeping, Engineering, and Security.

Sherrell's framing: staffing should be driven by **both occupancy and luxury service expectations**, not room count alone — same philosophy as the Butler standard.

---

## Proposed Minimum Manning — 50% Occupancy (112 occupied rooms)

| Function | Position | FTE |
|---|---|---|
| Guest Experience Team | Guest Experience Agent / Concierge | 4 |
| PBX / Call Center | Operators (3 shifts: 7A–3P, 3P–11P, 11P–7A) | 4 (recommended minimum; true 24/7 coverage factor ≈ 4.2 to cover days off/vacation/sick) |
| **Total** | | **8 FTE** |

Guest Experience coverage at 50%: 2 on pre-arrival planning/VIP coordination, 2 on concierge desk/in-house guests.

## Proposed Minimum Manning — 70% Occupancy (157 occupied rooms)

| Function | Position | FTE |
|---|---|---|
| Guest Experience Team | Guest Experience Agent (Pre-Arrival) | 4 |
| Guest Experience Team | Guest Experience Concierge | 2 |
| PBX / Call Center | PBX Operators | 5 |
| PBX / Call Center | Relief / Swing Operator | 1 |
| **Total** | | **12 FTE** |

Guest Experience coverage at 70%: dedicated pre-arrival specialist, concierge coverage during peak guest hours, supervisor manages VIPs and service recovery. PBX coverage adds meal-break coverage, peak call support, vacation/sick coverage on top of the base 24-hour shifts.

## Observations for the Unifocus build

- Document only gives two occupancy anchor points (50%, 70%) — no explicit 90–100% ceiling given, unlike the Butler standard work which built out full breakpoint tables across occupancy bands. Will likely need a similar breakpoint exercise once this becomes a live Unifocus standard.
- PBX/Call Center staffing is not occupancy-scaled the same way Guest Experience is — the 24-hour shift structure is a fixed floor (3 shifts/day) regardless of occupancy; only the vacation/sick/relief buffer and peak-call support flex with occupancy (4 → 6 FTE from 50% to 70%).
- This is a **separate labor standard track from Butlers** — Guest Relations Associates (Dept 108, per the 7/23 and 8/12 restructuring calls) absorb the old Concierge + PBX + Guest Relations Coordinator functions, while Butlers (Dept 107) absorb Guest Relations Coordinators into the junior butler tier. Worth confirming with Emre/Sherrell that PBX is definitely rolling into the same Dept 108 structure as Concierge, since PBX wasn't explicitly mentioned in the 7/23/8/12 department-merge discussions.
