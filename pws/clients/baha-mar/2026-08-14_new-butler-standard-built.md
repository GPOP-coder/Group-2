## As-built standard

| Shift | Driver KBI | Floor | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0800–1600 (AM)** | 01-RW Hotel Rooms (switched from Occupancy % — see note below) | 4 shifts (30.00 hrs) at 1–45 rooms | 20 shifts (150.00 hrs) at 214+ rooms |
| **1000–1800 Lobby Host** | 01-RW Hotel Rooms | 0 below 134 rooms | 1 shift (7.50 hrs) at 134+ rooms |
| **1500–2300 (PM)** | 01-RW Hotel Rooms | 4 shifts (30.00 hrs) at 1–45 rooms | 20 shifts (150.00 hrs) at 214+ rooms (same ramp as AM) |
| **2300–0700 (Overnight)** | 01-RW Hotel Rooms | 1 shift (7.50 hrs) at 1–111 rooms | 3 shifts (22.50 hrs) at 168+ rooms |

**AM/PM ramp:** steps up roughly every 11 rooms, +1 shift (+7.50 hrs) per step, from 4 shifts up to 20 shifts across 17 volume-range tiers — a smooth, near-uniform ramp rather than the old standard's 3-tier jumps (3→4→6 shifts).

**Overnight ramp:** 1 shift (1–111 rooms) → 2 shifts (112–167 rooms) → 3 shifts (168+ rooms).

## Resolves two open items from prior notes

1. **Overnight floor-vs-ceiling tension, resolved by design, not by picking one side.** The 8/12 live call wanted "minimum 2, stepping to 3." Emre's follow-up email showed "1 shift" at the floor. This build uses **both**: floor = 1 (per the email, per Pete's most-recent-email-rules standard), ramping up to **3** at high occupancy (satisfying the call's intent) — via an intermediate step to 2 at 112 rooms (50% occupancy) and 3 at 168 rooms (75% occupancy, exactly Sherrell's suggested trigger on the call).
2. **Driver KBI inconsistency, resolved.** The old/current standard mixed Occupancy % (AM) and Hotel Rooms count (Lobby Host/PM/Overnight) — flagged in `2026-08-14_current-butler-standard-baseline.md`. This build standardizes **all four shifts on Hotel Rooms count.**

## Confirms Lobby Host trigger point

**134 rooms** matches Emre's email exactly (60% occupancy × 224 rooms = 134.4, rounds to 134) — no ambiguity here, direct match.
