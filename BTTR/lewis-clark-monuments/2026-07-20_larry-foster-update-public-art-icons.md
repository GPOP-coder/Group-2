# Lewis & Clark Interpretive Trail — Status Update (Public Art + Icons)

**Source:** Email thread, Larry Foster → Lauren Martin/Amy Cherko (Amplify Arts), cc: Kevin McCarthy, Keith Bystrom, Pete Castellano, Jim Becic, Pat Slaven
**Date:** July 20, 2026 (thread includes earlier Jan 28 and May 29, 2026 updates for context)

---

## New in this update

- **Public art element:** Kevin McCarthy (NPS) has agreed to manage the public art element of the project. Kevin has worked with Amplify Arts staff before and will reach out to them soon. This resolves the "Amplify Arts role" open item from the May 29 update — NPS (Kevin) is taking point on public art coordination rather than Amplify Arts directly, though Amplify Arts remains in the loop (Lauren Martin, Amy Cherko now added to the thread).
- **Icons:** Following the Stakeholders meeting, the Steering Committee recommended moving forward. Pat Slaven and Larry selected **HGM Associates**, met with them to review Pat's Condition Analysis, and developed the proposal — **approved by the BTTR Board Friday 7/17/26** (matches [2026-07-17_board-meeting-minutes.md](../2026-07-17_board-meeting-minutes.md)).
- **HGM scope per Larry's recap:** Inspect each icon (except two excluded — see discrepancy note below), determine cleaning/repair/sealing approach, develop an engineer's estimate for BTTR review/approval, prepare bid documents, distribute to 3+ qualified contractors, run the bid process, and provide construction management once a contractor is selected.
- **HGM will begin work within 5 days** of Larry's email (i.e., by ~7/25/26).

## ⚠️ Discrepancy to resolve

Larry's email names the two excluded icon sites as **"Boyers Chute and the Childrens Wall."** The actual signed HGM agreement (`../2026-07-16_hgm-lcit-phase1-agreement.md`, line 56) names them as **"Boyer Chute and Haworth Park."** Boyer Chute matches; "Childrens Wall" vs. "Haworth Park" do not obviously match — could be a colloquial/alternate name for the same site, or a slip in Larry's recap. Confirm with Larry or Pat next contact; treat the signed contract as authoritative until clarified.
