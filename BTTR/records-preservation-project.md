## Background

BTTR has 25+ years of paper records (earliest from 2001) scattered across member organizations' offices, basements, and — most recently — the family of a founding member who passed away. There's no BTTR office; the board meets ~4x/year in person. Records were never centrally maintained because BTTR is a coalition of federal/state/county/city/neighborhood-level organizations plus some nonprofit members, each with their own office/staff resources (or lack thereof) and turnover.

**The motivation is not organization for its own sake** — it's that BTTR has had significant, underappreciated impact on the Omaha/Council Bluffs metro riverfront, and the scattered records hold stories worth preserving. This directly feeds the [Bob Kerrey Pedestrian Bridge book project](book-project/project-status.md), which needs a common access point for source documents and a shared timeline across authors.
