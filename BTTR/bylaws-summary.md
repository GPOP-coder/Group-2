# Back to the River, Inc. — Bylaws Summary

**Source:** "BTTR 2024 Bylaws.pdf," provided by Pete 2026-07-08. Adoption date on the signature page was left blank in the source document ("adopted by the Board as of ____ 2024") — unsigned/undated copy.

Full text not reproduced here; this is a working summary of governance provisions most relevant to board management. Refer to the original PDF for exact legal language.

## Board Composition (Article II §3-4)

- **20 Directors total**, matching [board-roster.md](board-roster.md):
  - 8 Local & Regional Public Jurisdictions Representatives (Douglas County, Sarpy County, NE Game & Parks, Papio-Missouri River NRD, Bellevue, Blair, Council Bluffs, Omaha)
  - 3 Organization Representatives (Fontenelle Forest, Bob Kerrey, MECA/Tri-Park Complex)
  - 9 At-Large Directors, elected by the Board
- Board can change the total number, but never below 3, and never in a way that shortens a sitting Director's term.

## Director Terms — no fixed expiration dates

**Key finding (2026-07-08):** The bylaws do not set term lengths or expiration dates for any director.

- **Representative Directors** serve for as long as they hold the institutional position they represent — e.g., whoever is Douglas County Board Chairperson holds that seat. Ends only on: earlier removal, resignation, death, or incapacity — or when they no longer hold the underlying position (automatic turnover, not a scheduled term end).
- **At-Large Directors** serve from appointment "until such Director's successor has been duly elected and qualified" or earlier removal/resignation/death/incapacity. No calendar term.

**Implication for the roster:** The "Confirmed"/reappointment dates on [board-roster.md](board-roster.md) mark when someone was (re)appointed, not a countdown to expiration. There is nothing in the bylaws to calculate an expiration date from — a director is current until one of the end conditions above actually happens.

## Removal & Vacancies (Article II §5-7)

- **Resignation:** written notice to the Board, presiding officer, Chair, or Secretary; effective on receipt unless a later date is specified.
- **Representative Director removal:** happens automatically when they no longer hold the represented position; can also be removed by amending the Articles or Bylaws.
- **At-Large Director removal:** can be removed *without cause* by Board resolution at any regular or special meeting.
- **Vacancies:** filled at the Board's discretion per Article II §4, at any regular or special meeting.

## Meetings (Article II §8-13)

- **Regular meetings:** quarterly, one of which is the annual organizational meeting (officer/committee appointments, business).
- **Special meetings:** callable by the President, presiding officer, or 20%+ of sitting Directors; minimum 2 days' notice (oral notice OK if reasonable).
- **Special-notice actions** (7+ days written notice required): director removal, director conflict-of-interest transactions, indemnification of officers/employees/agents, Articles amendments, Bylaws amendments, merger, non-ordinary-course asset sales, dissolution.
- **Quorum:** one-third of Directors.
- **Action:** majority vote of Directors present at a quorate meeting (higher threshold only where Articles/Bylaws/Act require it).
- Telecommunication participation is expressly allowed and counts as "present."

## Executive Committee & Officers (Article III)

- **Executive Committee:** 6 members, appointed by majority Board vote, serving at the Board's pleasure — President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, + 2 additional members.
- Board can also appoint additional VPs, assistant secretaries, assistant treasurers, etc. as needed. One person may hold multiple offices.
- **President:** CEO of the Corporation; presides at Board meetings; executes contracts/instruments on the Corporation's behalf unless limited by Board resolution.
- **Vice President:** acts for the President in their absence/incapacity/refusal to act.
- **Secretary:** records/keeps minutes, custodian of the corporate seal, handles required filings and notices.
- **Treasurer:** custody of funds/securities, accurate books, reports to Board on request, may be bonded if Board requires, assists with audits.
- **Assistant officers:** serve under their respective principal officer (Secretary/Treasurer) and ultimately the President.
- **No compensation** for Board service (Article III §... / confirmed under "Compensation").

## Records Requirements (Article VI)

Corporation must keep at its Principal Office: Articles of Incorporation (+ amendments), Bylaws (+ amendments), a list of current Directors with business/home addresses, and its most recent required filings with the Nebraska Secretary of State. Relevant to the ongoing [BTTR records preservation project](../CLAUDE.md).

## Other Notes

- **Fiscal year:** set by Board resolution, not fixed in the bylaws itself.
- **Amendments:** Bylaws or Articles can be amended by majority vote of the Board then in office, with proper special notice.
- **Conflict resolution order:** Nebraska nonprofit Act > Articles of Incorporation > these Bylaws.
