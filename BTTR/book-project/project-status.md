## Project Vision (Pete's)

> "Lots of beautiful pictures, pre-construction, construction, post construction, some text that people may or not read. A tribute to the bridge, the people who made it happen and especially Bob Kerrey. The bridge is a physical landmark that signifies the best we can do when a few wise people have a vision we work together to make it happen."

**Format:** Visual storytelling (coffee table aesthetic) + Narrative + Possibly Political Science case study framing  
**Audience:** Upscale Omaha-area gift shops, bridge stakeholders/workers, fundraiser for BTTR  
**Distribution:** Donor gift, giveaway to contributors, potential BTTR fundraiser with sponsorship underwriting

## Key Narrative Threads (to Track Across Interviews)

**Pete's Emphasis:**
1. **Elections matter; post-election is action time** — Personal decisions create policy windows
2. **Bridge as metaphor & connector** — Cities, counties, states, agencies, political parties, rivals
3. **Multi-level governance** — Federal, state, local agencies coordinating
4. **Obstacle-overcoming through collaboration** — Designers + builders solving complex problems

**From Bob's Interview:**
1. Rivers as unifying force connecting rural/urban Nebraska
2. Leadership through delegation — let competent people execute
3. Multi-agency complexity — ~12–13 agencies, built-in silo incentives
4. Bridge as metaphor — crossing, change, unlikely partnerships across divides
5. Staying young at heart — avoiding cynicism in politics
