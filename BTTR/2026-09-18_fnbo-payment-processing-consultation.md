# BTTR — FNBO Customer Consultation: Digital Donation/Payment Acceptance (Clover vs. PayPal)

**Date:** September 18, 2026, ~3:56 PM
**Attendees:** Pete Castellano (BTTR President); First National Bank of Omaha (FNBO) representative (name not captured on transcript — "Speaker 1"; follow-up email pending, add name once known) — **FNBO is where BTTR holds all its accounts**, confirmed 9/18/26.
**Source:** Plaud transcript, `09-18 Customer Consultation_ Back to the River - Digital Donation Acceptance via Clover vs PayPal`

**Why this call happened:** Directly triggered by the stuck **National Park Service invoice** ($2,450, Lewis & Clark Interpretive Trail Exhibit — see `BTTR/invoices/2026-09-18_NPS-invoice-2450-amplify-arts.md`). Pete flagged 9/18/26 that receiving payment from NPS is "a problem" — BTTR has no way to accept anything but a mailed check today, and that may not even be the right instrument for a federal disbursement. This call is also the direct answer to the earlier open question this session about whether backtotheriver.org has any donation mechanism (it doesn't) — BTTR literally has no digital payment acceptance infrastructure at all right now.

---

## Context going in

- BTTR is **entirely volunteer-run** — no office, no phone number, no dedicated staff. Pete's own words: "We really don't have much of an infrastructure."
- Historically checks-only. That's worked, but Pete wants BTTR to "operate in the real financial world of the day, and not just rely on checks."
- **Banked with FNBO since inception** — Pete's default preference is to stay with FNBO if the numbers work, but he's explicitly going to price-check PayPal first.

---

## What FNBO/Clover offers

| Method | Rate |
|---|---|
| Card-present (chip reader, e.g. Clover Go) | 2.7% + $0.10/transaction |
| Key-entered / online / virtual terminal / payment links / QR codes | 3.5% + $0.10/transaction |

- **Clover Go mobile reader:** ~$135 + tax/shipping — lowest-cost device option.
- **No-device options:** QR codes (for event signage/marketing), a payment button on a website, or an emailed/texted payment link — all route through the 3.5% + $0.10 online rate.
- **Monthly minimum applies** (amount not stated on the call) regardless of activity.
- **Account closure:** no penalty to close after "two or three months" if it's not a fit — minimum monthly fees still apply while open, but no early-termination penalty.
- Rep offered to follow up by email with the Clover account setup details, fee schedule, device options, and QR/payment-link mechanics — sent to **pete.castellano@gmail.com** (Pete confirmed that address is already on BTTR's FNBO account).

## PayPal — Pete's stated alternative to check

- Pete's framing: PayPal is simpler in structure — "you just tell them about the bank account, and you accept the incoming payment because they're the receiver, they're the ones that collect it and then disperse."
- Pete asked about a trial period; not resolved on this call — he's going to research PayPal's own rates/setup independently before deciding.
- No PayPal numbers were captured on this call — this is homework Pete is doing separately, not something FNBO's rep provided.

---

## Where this stands

Pete has not committed to either path. His own summary on the call: "I'll pursue PayPal just to see what that looks like, but we've been with FNBO since our inception, and if I'd like to do it through you guys, let me take a look at what it looks like through PayPal, just to be certain."

**Confirmed, per the actual NPS email thread (Pete forwarded it 9/18/26 evening) — this is not a check/EFT-vs-card ambiguity, and it's not an unusual funding source either.** Kevin McCarthy's original 9/16/26 email states NPS's requirement plainly: invoice + completed Form 889 + **"ability to pay with cc or paypal,"** due before end of September 2026. That's simply how NPS needs to pay this specific $2,450 — Pete's earlier theory (credit card refund, program surplus) wasn't it. Full thread, including Larry Foster's fallback plan to route the payment through Papio-Missouri River NRD (P-MRNRD) if BTTR isn't ready in time: [2026-09-18 NPS Payment Routing — Amplify Arts Funds](2026-09-18_nps-payment-routing-amplify-arts-funds.md).

**The invoice has already been sent** (9/18/26, 4:48 PM, with Form 889 attached) — this Clover-vs-PayPal decision is now about actually being able to *collect* the payment before end of month, not about whether to send the invoice at all.
