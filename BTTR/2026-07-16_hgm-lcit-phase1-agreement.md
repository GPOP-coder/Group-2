## Scope of Services (Exhibit A)
Based on HGM's meeting with Larry Foster and Pat Slaven on July 13, 2026.

**I. Preliminary Design**
- Visit and photograph all 8 icon locations, note existing conditions:
  1. N.P. Dodge Park, Omaha
  2. Levi Carter Park, Omaha
  3. Miller's Landing, Omaha
  4. Lewis & Clark Landing, Omaha (includes review of a cast stone compass in the pavement)
  5. Lewis & Clark Monument, Council Bluffs
  6. Dodge Riverside Park, Council Bluffs
  7. Western Historic Trails Center, Council Bluffs
  8. Lauritzen Garden, Omaha
- Prepare preliminary construction documents — each location treated as a separate part of the overall project, with its own list of required improvements, supported by photos/notes/drawings.
- Notes to save & protect existing artwork during the improvement process (artwork renovation itself is a future phase, not this contract).
- Contact a cast stone manufacturer re: cleaning, repair, and sealing options.
- Develop an Opinion of Probable Costs (OPC).
- Hold a Preliminary Review Meeting with BTTR staff to review plans and OPC.

**II. Draft Final Construction Documents (90%)** — near-complete plans/specs; general guidelines to clean/repair/seal each icon; bid documents set up as **line-item bids per location** so BTTR can choose all or specific areas based on budget; updated OPC; Final Review Meeting.

**III. Final Construction Documents** — finalize plans/specs for bidding upon BTTR approval.

**IV. Bidding** — send to minimum 3 contractors; answer questions via addenda; host bid opening at HGM's Council Bluffs office; provide a Letter of Recommendation; prepare/send construction contracts to the selected contractor.

**V. Construction Administration** — Pre-Construction Meeting; bi-weekly (minimum) construction meetings with notes/photos distributed; review shop drawings/submittals; review and sign Contractor pay applications, forward to BTTR; issue change orders if authorized; final walkthrough per icon with BTTR and the contractor to develop punch lists; closeout documents with final pay application.

## General Provisions (Exhibit B) — Notable Terms
- HGM retains ownership of instruments of service (plans, specs, field data, calculations) for 10 years; BTTR gets a paper copy for reference only, not for reuse on other projects.
- Termination: either party may terminate on 7 days' written notice; HGM compensated for services performed prior to termination/suspension.
- Limitation of liability: capped at the greater of HGM's total compensation received or $100,000.
- Construction staking liability separately capped at the greater of compensation for that service or $5,000.
- Opinions of Probable Cost are HGM's professional judgment only — no warranty that actual bids/costs will match.
- Jobsite safety is the General Contractor's responsibility, not HGM's; HGM to be named additional insured on the GC's general liability policy.
- Disputes go to non-binding mediation.
