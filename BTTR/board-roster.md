# Back to the River — Board Composition, Officers and Directors

**Source:** Board roster spreadsheet provided by Pete, 2026-07-06, corrected the same day per Pete's direct confirmation. **This is the current, authoritative BTTR board list** — the two flagged items below are the only known discrepancies versus the source spreadsheet, both resolved.

**Governance reference:** See [bylaws-summary.md](bylaws-summary.md) for board size, meeting cadence, officer duties, removal/vacancy rules, and the Executive Committee mapping.

**Terms/expiration (resolved 2026-07-08):** Per the [bylaws](bylaws-summary.md) (Article II §4), directors do **not** have fixed terms or expiration dates. Representative Directors serve for as long as they hold the institutional position they represent; At-Large Directors serve until a successor is elected or they resign/are removed/die/become incapacitated. The "Confirmed" column below is an appointment or reappointment date only — there is no expiration date to calculate. A seat is current until one of those end conditions actually occurs.

**Known outdated items (do not treat as current):**
- **President:** Roster lists Clare Duda as President. Pete Castellano has been President since **November 2025**. Clare Duda **remains an active At Large board member** — only the President title on this row is stale, confirmed by Pete 2026-07-06.
- **Gayle Malmquist (At Large, listed as Treasurer):** Gayle Malmquist is deceased — BTTR dedicated a memorial bench to her in the [Council Bluffs Rivers Edge benches project](../BTTR/2026_0613_Banking_Status_and_Treasurer_Update.md). She was BttR President 2004–2010 and a founding member. **Her At Large seat was filled by David Dover, elected Treasurer unanimously at the July 17, 2026 board meeting.**
- **Pete Castellano's row** lists him as "Assistant Secretary" — superseded by his November 2025 Presidency.
