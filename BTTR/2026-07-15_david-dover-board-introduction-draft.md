Hi Board Members,

I'm pleased to introduce David Dover as a candidate for BTTR Treasurer, with a vote scheduled at this Friday's board meeting.

David has spent nearly his entire career in and around public service, and I think you will find he brings exactly what we need in a new board member.

About David:

- **Military service.** David served 13 years in the U.S. Navy Reserve in Signals Intelligence and Tactical Information Operations.
- **Service to elected officials.** He was a Policy Advisor on Mayor Fahey's Transition Team and later Deputy Chief of Staff for Mayor Suttle, under Chief of Staff Steve Oltmans.
