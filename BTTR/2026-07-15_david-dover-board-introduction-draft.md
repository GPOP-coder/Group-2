Board Members,

I'm pleased to introduce David Dover as our candidate for BTTR Treasurer, with a vote scheduled at Thursday's board meeting.

David came to my attention through Gary DiSilvestro, who knows us both well. What Gary didn't know — and what made this feel almost meant to be — is that David and I have history. He interned in Senator Bob Kerrey's Omaha office when I was on staff there, and later joined the transition team when I was leaving Mayor Daub's office and Mayor Fahey was coming in. He's been part of the civic fabric of this community for a long time, and now he's being asked to help steward an organization that grew directly from that era of Omaha's riverfront story.

**About David:**

David is currently COO and President of Operations and Government Affairs at Lifespan Mental Health in Omaha, a behavioral health organization he co-founded with his wife Laura in 2021 and has grown to a team of more than 20 professionals. Before that he spent four years leading government affairs and policy at Omaha Public Power District, served as Deputy Chief of Staff for the City of Omaha, and completed 13 years in the U.S. Navy Reserve in signals intelligence and tactical information operations. He holds degrees in Political Science, International Relations, and Public Administration from UNO.

David brings exactly the combination of operational discipline, civic leadership, and executive experience we need in a Treasurer — someone who understands how organizations are built and run, and who is deeply rooted in this community.

Thursday will be your opportunity to meet David. I'd encourage you to arrive a few minutes early if you can — I think you'll come away feeling the same way I do about this candidacy.

If you have any questions before the meeting, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly.

Looking forward to seeing everyone at the meeting.

Pete Castellano
