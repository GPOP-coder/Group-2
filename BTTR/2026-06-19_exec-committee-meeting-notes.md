## 2. Digital Storage

- BTTR's registered agent has always been the NRD — no change needed there
- Michelle knows of a service that bundles registered agent + document storage portal (~$100/year); email-agnostic (no Gmail or Outlook required); she's currently testing it with another org
- **Google:** Mark's Audubon uses Google Docs — works well, no pushback on Gmail requirement

- **Key risk of Google:** Documents tied to a Gmail account are lost if that account is deleted
- **Solution:** Create position-based accounts (not person-based) — e.g., admin@bttr.org — password resets when person leaves; documents stay with the org
- **Action: Michelle will bring a proposal with 2–3 options to July 17 meeting**
- Preference leaning toward Google; Michelle is familiar with administering it
