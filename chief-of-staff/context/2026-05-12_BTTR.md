---

## ORIGIN STORY

Back to the River grew out of Senator Bob Kerrey's initiative in the mid-to-late 1990s to bring together local, state, and federal resources that had authority and money but were working in silos. Pete was Kerrey's staffer and attended most coalition meetings.

After Kerrey left office in January 2001, Pete formally incorporated the nonprofit. Pete continued the riverfront work through Mayor Daub's office (2001), then formally took leadership of BTTR after leaving Unifocus.

**Pete's unique position:** He was present at virtually every chapter — Senate staff when coalition formed, Mayor's office when Bob Kerrey Bridge builders presented bids, now as President producing a book about the bridge.
