**Mission:** Support transplant recipients, candidates, donor families, caregivers, and promote organ donor awareness in the Omaha/Lincoln/Council Bluffs area.

---

## WHAT THEY DO

- Provide moral support and information to pre- and post-transplant families
- Promote public awareness of transplant success and need for donors
- Honor donor families
- Host monthly meetings at Live On Nebraska, 3867 Leavenworth St., Omaha
- Provide small financial grants to transplant families in need
- Podcast, videos, social media presence
- Annual fundraising events

**Kim Castellano** fundraised for NTSG at her 2025 heartversary party — raffle tickets and heart-themed specialty drinks. NTSG members attended the celebration alongside medical professionals.
