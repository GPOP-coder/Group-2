# Kim's Timeline Log — Date, Time, Place

*Started July 22, 2026. Companion to [timeline-log.md](timeline-log.md) (Pete's own), built from receipts and documents that are Kim's, not Pete's. Lighter-weight — won't have anywhere near the volume or detail of Pete's log, since it depends on old paperwork surfacing rather than a live capture habit. But it doubles as raw material for [Kim's book](kim-heart-book.md) — a real date, place, and who-was-there detail from decades ago is exactly the kind of texture a memoir needs and memory alone won't reliably hold onto.*

Same loose, flat, chronological format as Pete's log — one entry per event, fields added as the source provides them.

---

## Entry Format

```
## YYYY-MM-DD

**Time:** (if known)
**Place:** Vendor/location name, city, state
**Source:** Receipt / Document / other
**Notes:** (optional)

---
```
