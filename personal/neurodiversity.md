Personal exploration of how Pete's brain works, reframed from liability to asset.

## The Diagnosis Context

Pete may have **ADHD-Inattentive** — unconfirmed, but consistent with lifelong patterns. The ADHD-Inattentive presentation tends to be quieter and less obvious than hyperactive ADHD: it shows up as difficulty sustaining focus on low-interest tasks, getting pulled into tangents, losing track of details, and going blank when the stimulation stops. But it also correlates with pattern recognition, big-picture synthesis, and bursts of intense focused output when something is genuinely engaging.

CLAUDE.md flags Pete as "tangent-prone" and notes the goal: **leverage the superpowers (live insight, big-picture thinking, pattern recognition), not just compensate for the difficulties.**

## Integrative Intelligence

**Source:** "Always Go Off On Tangents? You May Have This Type Of Intelligence" — Amy Glover, HuffPost UK, 08/23/2026
**Link:** https://apple.news/ALFoKygOLTi2TA2EFxjfp3Q

**Key concept:** Integrative intelligence — part of the Triad of Intelligence (cognitive, emotional, integrative) — is the ability to connect seemingly unrelated things. Linked to analogical reasoning: applying the rules of one field to another. Called the "rarest" form of intelligence.

Psychologist Lee Chambers (CEO, Male Allies UK): *"It's fair to say that modern life doesn't often support this type of thinking and the practice of it."*

**Pete's read:** The tangent isn't the bug — it's the signal. The mind that wanders to a connection isn't losing the thread; it's finding one that most people would never see. The CLAUDE.md liability ("tangent-prone, Claude should gently redirect") and the integrative intelligence asset are the same trait observed from two different angles.

**Where it shows up in Pete's work:**
- Walking into a casino and immediately seeing the Unifocus labor model application
- Political operative background informing how he reads hotel organizational dynamics
- Photography-as-memory-storage philosophy crossing into thoughts about grief, objects, and letting go (see `personal/stuff-philosophy.md`)
- Building a "Chief of Staff agent" while most people are still learning to use ChatGPT

**The tension:** Billable-hour discipline, ADHD-Inattentive pull, and modern life's demand for linear task completion all push against this mode of thinking. The challenge isn't suppressing it — it's building structures (Clockify, morning planning, COS agent) that hold the frame while the integrative mind does what it does.
