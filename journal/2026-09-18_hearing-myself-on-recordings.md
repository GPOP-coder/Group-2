# Hearing Myself on Recordings

**Date:** September 18, 2026

Checking the Plaud audio today (chasing down a missed voicemail transcription for Genevieve Kelley) turned into something more than a troubleshooting task. Listening back to myself on recordings — not just this one, but the accumulating habit of it since Plaud came into the workflow — is making me more aware of my own speech patterns, diction, pace.

It wasn't the reason I got the recorder. Plaud was supposed to solve the lost-breakthrough problem: the live client moment where the explanation crystallizes, then evaporates before I can write it down. That's still the main job it does. But there's a second thing happening underneath it — I don't normally get to hear myself the way other people hear me. Now I do, regularly, almost as a byproduct of the capture habit rather than something I set out to build.

Worth paying attention to as it develops — not sure yet what I do with it, just noticing it's happening.
